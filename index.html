The best moments of startup & AI podcasts, in under 60 seconds.

shortlane is an independent media account. We take long conversations that founders and creators license to us through official clipping campaigns, cut the strongest moment, add captions, and publish it as a vertical short on our own channels.

How it works

1. Licensed sourcesOnly podcasts and creators who explicitly allow clipping through campaigns such as Whop Content Rewards. No unauthorized content.
2. Find the momentEach episode is transcribed and scored. We keep the 15–60 s segments with a real hook: a number, a strong opinion, a founder story.
3. Cut & captionVertical crop, word-by-word captions, a title card and the partner mention required by the campaign.
4. PublishOur tool, shortlane clipper, posts the finished clip to our own YouTube and TikTok accounts through their official APIs.

About the tool

shortlane clipper is the in-house tool behind this account. It runs on our own computer, is used by a single person (the account owner) and only publishes to the shortlane accounts. It does not offer sign-in, collect data from viewers or manage other people's accounts. Details: Terms of Service · Privacy Policy.
