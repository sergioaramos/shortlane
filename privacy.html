Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

shortlane clipper ("the App") is an internal tool used only by the owner of the shortlane social media accounts. This policy explains what data the App handles.

1. Data we collect

The App stores only the OAuth access and refresh tokens that the platforms (TikTok, YouTube) issue when the account owner authorizes it, plus the basic profile information returned by that authorization (account id and display name). Tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never shared.

2. Data we do not collect

The App does not collect data from viewers, followers or any other user. It does not use cookies, analytics or tracking on this website.

3. Use of data

Tokens are used exclusively to upload videos to the owner's own accounts and to read the statistics of those videos.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. Data is transmitted only to the platform APIs that issued it.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept while the App is in use and can be revoked at any time from the platform's account settings (TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions; Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions). Revoking access removes the App's ability to use them; local copies are deleted on request.
